Other pharmacologic treatments

                                                              i.      Roflumilast => MOA pde4 inhibitor

1.      Place in therapy => severe COPD despite triple therapy in pts with eos < 100 (so basically if these patients cannot get ICS because ICS cut off is > 100 EOS? )

2.      Drug interactions => use with strong cyp inducers is not recommended

                                                            ii.      Azithromycin

1.      Dosing => Daily azithro 250 daily x 1 year was found to lengthen time to first exacerbation

2.      Dosing => Azithro 500 mg Three times a week (and erythromycin 500 mg BID) have reduced exacerbations

3.      Place in therapy => add to LABA/LAMA + or – ICS if eos < 100 and former smoker (basically like roflumilast but also add in FORMER SMOKER)

                                                          iii.      Vaccinations in COPD => tdap, covid19, shingles

                                                          iv.      Alpha-1 antitrypsin augmentation therapy

1.      Drug => add alpha1 proteinase inhibitor

2.      Recommended for =>  young pts with severe hereditary alpha 1 antitrypsin deficiency and established emphysema

                                                            v.      Therapies

1.      Home oxygen therapy => recommended for pts with SaO2 88% or less, or PaO2 of 55 mHg or less

2.      Beta blockers should not be withheld in pts with COPD who have heart disease, CHF, or other CV conditions in which these agent is used

a.      Help by upregulating beta 2 receptors (doing what) in the lungs which may improve effectiveness of beta agonists

3.      Statins have no good evidence to provide clinical benefit in pts with COPD WITHOUT other CV disease risk factors

                                                          vi.      Management of COPD exacerbations

1.      Classification of COPD exacerbations and how to treat

a.      Mild:SA bronchodilators only

b.     Moderate: SA bronchodilators PLUS antibiotics AND/OR CS

c.      Severe or ED visits:

                                                                                                                                      i.      No respiratory failure: RR 20-30, no mental status changes, hypoxemia improves with O2, PaCO2 does not increase

                                                                                                                                    ii.      ARF, non -life threatening: >30 RR, hypoxemia still improves with O2, PaCO2 increased

                                                                                                                                  iii.      ARF life-threatening: > 30 RR, acute mental status changes, hypoxemia not improving with supplemental O2, PaCO2 increases, leading to reduction in pH

2.      Goal O2 is 88-92% (excessive O2 can blunt hypoxic drive and worsen acidosis)

3.      Treatment for COPD exacerbation: albuterol + ipratropium + steroids (prednisone dose as high as 60 mg have been used) for 5 days

4.      Antibiotic treatment

a.      In patients with GOLD 3 or 4 severity, this organism pseudomonas aeruginosa should be considered a potential pathogen

b.     Antibiotics recommended for 2+ cardinal symptoms out of 3:(what are the 3 symptoms) dyspnea, sputum volume, sputum purulence

 

Vaccines

1.      Live vaccines should be avoided in patients who are severely immunocompromised or pregnant women (theoretic risk to fetus)

2.      pneumococcal

a.      When to recommended pneumococcal vaccination: pts > 65 yo or 19-64 yo with comorbidities

b.     Space at least 8 weeks between PCV15 and PPSV 23 (in adults who are immunocompromised) or 1 year in adults who are not immunocompromised

3.      Influenza

a.      recombinant or cell culture based inactive vaccine do not contain egg protein

b.     if egg or egg containing food reaction is hives => safe to give still

4.      herpes zoster vaccine

a.      when to recommend: >50 yo or >19 yo if immunocompromised

5.      HPV vaccine

a.      When to recommend: males and females 9-45 yo of age; while 27-45 yo this is shared decision making
